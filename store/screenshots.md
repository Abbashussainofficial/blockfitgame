# Screenshots to capture – Block Fit

Google Play phone screenshots: **2–8 images** (upload at least 4, ideally 8),
PNG or JPEG, portrait **9:16**, each side between 320 px and 3840 px.
**1080 × 1920** or **1080 × 2400** works well. Use a phone or emulator with
the status bar clean (full battery, no notifications). Capture with
`adb exec-out screencap -p > shot.png` or the device's screenshot button.

Use a real-looking mid-game board (score of a few hundred, several colours),
not an empty one. Recommended order (first 3 matter most):

| # | Screen | What to show | Suggested caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Game – dragging | A piece mid-drag with the **green ghost** preview and a row about to complete (tinted) | "Drag, drop and fit!" |
| 2 | Game – line clear | The flash/shrink of 2+ lines clearing with **"COMBO x2!"** visible | "Clear lines and build combos" |
| 3 | Home | Logo, high score, Play / Continue buttons | "Relaxing puzzle – no timer" |
| 4 | Themes | Theme grid with Classic Dark selected and one locked theme | "Unlock 5 beautiful themes" |
| 5 | Game – light theme | A game in **Sunset** or **Forest** | "Play your way" |
| 6 | Game – Neon theme | Busy board in Neon | "Chase your high score" |
| 7 | Game Over | Score with the **NEW BEST!** badge | "Beat your best" |
| 8 | Settings | Sound/vibration toggles, Remove Ads | "Works offline. Play anywhere." |

Tips
- Captions are optional. If you add them, overlay them in a design tool, not
  in the app.
- Don't show test ads in screenshots (they read "Test Ad"). Turn on
  airplane mode, or buy Remove Ads with a license-tester account, before
  capturing.
- Optional: a 7-inch and a 10-inch tablet screenshot set if you enable
  tablet distribution.
- Feature graphic (required, 1024 × 500): store/graphics/feature-graphic-1024x500.png
  (placeholder).
- App icon (512 × 512): store/graphics/icon-512.png (placeholder).
