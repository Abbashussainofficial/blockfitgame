## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Block Fit is a calm, satisfying block puzzle you can play anytime, anywhere – even offline.

Drag colourful pieces onto the 8×8 board and fill complete rows or columns to clear them. There is no timer and nothing falls, so you can think through every move. The game ends only when none of your three pieces fits on the board.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag a piece from the tray onto the board.
• A preview shows exactly where it will land: green means it fits.
• Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
• Clear several lines at once for a multi-line bonus.
• Clear lines on consecutive turns to build a COMBO multiplier.
• Plan ahead: when no piece fits, the game is over.

FEATURES
• Over 35 block shapes: lines, squares, L, J, T, S and Z pieces, corners and more
• Smart piece generation that is always fair, with difficulty that rises slowly as you score
• Combo streaks, multi-line bonuses and a "New Best!" celebration
• Undo a move when you slip, or revive once per game for a second chance
• 5 beautiful themes – Classic Dark, Ocean, Sunset, Forest and Neon – unlocked as you play
• Smooth animations, satisfying sounds and haptic feedback (each can be turned off)
• Your game saves automatically, so you can continue right where you left off
• Works fully offline – no internet connection needed to play
• Light on battery and storage

Relax, train your brain and chase your high score. How many lines can you clear?

Block Fit is free to play and supported by ads. A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase removes banner and between-game ads. Optional reward videos (for Revive and extra Undo) stay available if you want them.
